古镇概况 Scenic Overview

惠山古镇坐落于无锡城西惠山山麓，是江南地区保存最完整、规模最大的祠堂古建筑群落，集山水风光、古建祠堂、千年泉文化、文人诗词于一体。景区无需大门票，自然清幽、古色古香，是江南小众人文旅游、研学采风、休闲漫步的绝佳目的地。 Located at the foot of Huishan Mountain in western Wuxi, Huishan Ancient Town boasts the most complete and largest group of ancient ancestral halls in Jiangnan. It integrates landscape scenery, ancient architecture, millennial spring culture and literati poetry. Free of entrance ticket, it is an excellent destination for cultural tourism, study tours and leisure walks.

景区核心地标天下第二泉，为唐代茶圣陆羽评定的天下名泉，因阿炳名曲《二泉映月》享誉海内外，是惠山古镇最具代表性的文化名片。 The core landmark, Huishan Spring (The Second Best Spring under Heaven), was rated by Lu Yu, the tea saint of the Tang Dynasty. Famous for Ah Bing's classic erhu music, it is the most iconic cultural symbol of Huishan Ancient Town.

古镇特色文化 Local Featured Culture

泉茶文化 Spring & Tea Culture

惠山泉为千古名泉，自唐代以来无数文人雅士在此品泉煮茶，形成独属于惠山的泉茶礼仪与雅集文化，苏轼、李绅等名家均留下传世诗篇。 Huishan Spring is a famous ancient spring. Since the Tang Dynasty, countless literati have tasted spring water and brewed tea here, forming unique spring tea culture with numerous classic poems left by famous scholars.

祠堂宗族文化 Ancestral Hall Culture

惠山古镇被誉为“江南祠堂博物馆”，百余座古祠堂跨越唐宋明清，记录江南宗族礼制、家风传承与古建筑营造技艺。 Known as the "Jiangnan Ancestral Hall Museum", Huishan Ancient Town has hundreds of ancient halls spanning multiple dynasties, recording traditional rituals, family inheritance and ancient construction techniques.

非遗民俗文化 Intangible Cultural Heritage

景区常年开展茶道体验、书画雅集、传统民乐、古镇写生等文化活动，传承江南传统非遗民俗，氛围感浓厚。 The scenic area regularly holds tea ceremonies, calligraphy salons, traditional music and sketching activities, inheriting traditional Jiangnan folk culture.

诗词人文底蕴 Poetry & Humanity Heritage

千年来文人墨客云集惠山，留下海量诗词碑刻，山水与人文相融，造就独树一帜的江南诗意古镇风貌。 For thousands of years, numerous scholars gathered in Huishan and left a large number of poems and inscriptions, forming a unique poetic ancient town style.
